❯❯❯❯Emergency Go Bag Essentials ✔

1. ✚ First Aid Kit ᝰ.ᐟ

♡ A first aid kit for an emergency bag should include bandages and gauze, antiseptic wipes, medical tape, and disposable gloves for wound care.

2. 𓌉◯𓇋 Emergency Foods and Water ᝰ.ᐟ

♡ Ensure that the food in your Go Bag is non-perishable. Include also a couple of water bottles just incase there are no available clean water sources.

3. ⚒ Emergency Tools ᝰ.ᐟ

♡ Includes a Flashlight, Batteries/Power Banks for Charging, and Whistles to call for attention.

4. 🪪 IDs and Emergency Contact Numbers ᝰ.ᐟ

♡ This is a key essential. Even photocopies of your IDs are good. Include pieces of paper that have your emergency contacts incase of emergency.
